# Late payment policy

Payment term is 30 days unless the invoice says otherwise. Count the days overdue from the due date.

| Days overdue | What we do | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 7 | Friendly reminder. Assume they forgot. | Light, no blame |
| 8 to 30 | Second reminder. Ask them to pay within 7 days. | Polite but clear |
| More than 30 | Last reminder. Say that we will pause deliveries until it is paid. | Firm, still polite |

Always include: invoice number, invoice date, amount, due date and our IBAN (NL00 BANK 0000 0000 00, example).
Never threaten legal steps or extra costs in a reminder; the owner decides that personally.
Regular customers who have paid on time for a year get one extra friendly reminder before the firm one.
