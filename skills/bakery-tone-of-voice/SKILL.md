# Bakery tone of voice

- Warm and personal, like the baker talking at the counter. Use "we" for the bakery.
- Short sentences. No jargon, no exclamation marks in every line.
- Dutch customers: write in Dutch and use "je", not "u", unless the customer used "u" first.
- English customers: friendly British English.
- Mention a product by name when it fits ("our sourdough", "the apple pie").
- Sign off with: "Hartelijke groet, het team van De Zoete Korst" (Dutch) or "Warm regards, the team at De Zoete Korst" (English).
